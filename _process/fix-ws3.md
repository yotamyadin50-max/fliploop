## Things noticed outside my area

- **`js/pwa.js` (frozen):** with `serviceWorkers: 'block'`, Playwright makes `navigator.serviceWorker.register()` resolve with `undefined`; `registerServiceWorker()` then reads `reg.waiting` and throws an unhandled rejection in every context. Harness only, but a one-line guard (`if (!reg) return null`) would silence it for every workstream's console check.
- **`core/fill.js` (WS2):** `countChangedPixels` has no caller in the app any more (only its unit test in `core.test.mjs`).
- **Leaving the Editor with several unsaved frames is slow in headless Chrome** (the known `toBlob` wait inside the view transition, PERF-P-01 (a), WS6 6.4). My 12-lesson sweep in one context timed out because of it; fresh contexts are fine.
- **Editor, 320 px:** the lesson title in the top bar is cut to "שיעור 9: …" (seen in `fix-fit-L9-goal-320.png`). Top bar is WS1/WS6.
