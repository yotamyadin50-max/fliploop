## Noticed outside my area

- `js/pwa.js` (frozen): with service workers blocked (rule 1.6), `navigator.serviceWorker.register()` resolves `undefined` in Playwright and `registerServiceWorker()` throws `Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'waiting')`, an unhandled rejection on every page load in every workstream's tests. A real browser never returns `undefined` there. I catch it in `boot()` (my function); a `if (!reg) return null` in `pwa.js` would be the cleaner fix.
- PERF-P-01 (a) is real in headless Chrome: Editor to Gallery with unsaved work took over 2 s inside the view transition (WS6 6.4). With saves at 250 ms it is hit far less often.
- Python's `http.server` (ENV-NOTE) drops connections under Chrome's module loads. Other workstreams may see "the app never started" for the same reason.
